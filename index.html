About

I am a researcher working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, fraud detection, and technology impact assessment and policy management. My work focuses on building explainable and responsible AI systems that can be deployed in high-stakes domains including banking, healthcare, retail and energy sector.

I completed my PhD at IIT Delhi, where I developed AI capability management framework for SMEs. I am currently a Visiting Scholar in the Fraud Research Group at Aston University, working on AI-driven solutions to romance crime with academic and industry partners.

My broader interests span responsible AI, technology adoption and management, explainability (XAI), and the application of machine learning to complex real-world problems. I am passionate about bridging rigorous research with meaningful societal impact.
