About

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Birmingham City University, working on the UKRI-funded SMART SIP+ project, an interdisciplinary initiative exploring innovative downstream uses of energy generated by solar irrigation pumps in Bangladesh. My current work examines technology adoption, energy utilisation, business feasibility, institutional conditions and pathways for scaling sustainable energy solutions.

I completed my PhD in Technology Management at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Delhi in March 2026. My doctoral research focused on AI capability management in small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), with particular attention to organisational capabilities, technology-related decision-making and the strategic adoption of artificial intelligence.

My research sits at the intersection of Responsible AI and AI governance, technology management, digital transformation, organisational capabilities, customer analytics and technology policy. I use interdisciplinary and mixed-method approaches, combining qualitative inquiry, systematic literature reviews, machine learning, natural language processing and multi-criteria decision-making methods to investigate complex organisational and societal challenges.

Alongside my research, I contribute to teaching, research supervision and executive education. I have taught and trained more than 5,000 learners across business analytics, machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, data mining and responsible AI, and have reviewed manuscripts for academic journals.

Research

My research investigates how organisations, communities and institutions adopt, govern and create value from emerging technologies. It brings together technology management, responsible innovation and data-driven approaches to address organisational and societal challenges.

Responsible AI & AI Governance

Examining organisational approaches to responsible AI, explainability, fairness, accountability, assurance and AI risk governance, particularly in high-impact settings such as financial services and healthcare.

AI Capabilities & Technology Management

Investigating how SMEs and other organisations develop, combine and deploy AI-related capabilities, and how strategic, organisational and environmental factors shape technology adoption and value creation.

Energy Innovation & Technology Adoption

Studying solar irrigation pump adoption and downstream energy utilisation in Bangladesh, including the institutional and PESTLE conditions, business models and feasibility of productive uses of surplus energy.

Data, Customer & Digital Analytics

Applying machine learning, deep learning, NLP and explainable methods to customer analytics, digital transformation, text-based decision support and data-driven organisational practice.

Technology Policy & Sustainable Development

Exploring how policy, institutions, markets and social conditions influence the diffusion, governance and inclusive use of technologies, with particular interest in emerging economies and South Asian contexts.

AI Methods, Privacy & Explainability

Developing and evaluating analytical approaches involving NLP, federated learning, privacy-aware machine learning and explainable AI to support trustworthy data-driven decisions.
